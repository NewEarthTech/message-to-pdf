Privacy

The short version: your messages never leave your Mac.

Sheaf is a local application. It reads your Messages database on your own computer to build a PDF, and that's the end of it. There is no account to create, nothing to sign in to, and no “sync.” Your conversations are never uploaded, transmitted, or seen by us or anyone else.

What Sheaf does on your Mac

  • Reads your local chat.db (with the Full Disk Access you grant) to list conversations and build the PDF you ask for.
  • Writes that PDF to the folder you choose.
  • That's it.

What Sheaf does not do

  • No account, login, or profile.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No network calls of its own — the app doesn't phone home, check licenses, or send your data anywhere.
  • No ads, and nothing sold or shared. There's nothing to sell, because we never receive your data.

Because Sheaf is open source, these aren't just promises — you can verify them in the code.

Buying Sheaf

The one place data leaves your Mac is checkout, and that's the purchase — not your messages. Payment is handled by our reseller, Paddle (Paddle.com Market Ltd), who acts as the merchant of record. Paddle collects what's needed to process the sale and send your receipt and download link (for example, your email and billing details) under their own privacy policy. Sheaf itself never sees or stores this — we only learn that a sale happened.

Support

If you email us for help, we see your email and whatever you choose to tell us. We never ask for your messages or your database, and we have no way to access them.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here. The core commitment — that Sheaf processes your messages only on your own Mac and uploads nothing — is not going to change.
