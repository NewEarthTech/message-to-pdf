## 0.9.0

Pre-1.0 baseline of Message to PDF — export one iMessage or SMS conversation from your Mac's `chat.db` to a PDF that looks like Messages. Signed, notarized, universal macOS app. Releases past this point are cut by the automated pipeline; a breaking-change commit graduates the version to 1.0.0.
